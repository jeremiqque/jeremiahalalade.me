## Overview

Campus Update brings official school news, urgent alerts, events and the academic calendar into one app, sent from the school straight to the student's phone. Every post is labelled, so students can see exactly who said it.

I am a co-founding design engineer on the product. This page covers the public website that explains the idea to students and schools and collects early-access sign-ups for the first pilot, with the Computer Science department at FOCIT.

## Challenge

On most campuses, news travels by forwarded screenshot. It reaches the last WhatsApp group after the deadline has passed. Registration and exam dates sit under hundreds of unread messages. And a screenshot has no source, so fake notices spread as fast as real ones.

The product needed to fix that, and the website needed to make the fix obvious in a few seconds: official, relevant to you, and on time.

## Outcome

The site opens with one line, "Everything important on campus. Verified.", next to a look at the admin dashboard schools publish from.

It then describes the problem the way students live it, and shows the answer in four small steps: a school admin publishes, the notice is targeted by faculty, department or level, a push alert arrives, and a clear label shows who sent it. A comparison of an official notice and an unsourced forward makes the point without a long explanation.

Features, a short FAQ and an early-access form for students, staff and school admins finish the page.
