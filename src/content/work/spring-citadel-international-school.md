A school running since 2008 with nothing online a parent could judge it by. A Facebook page and word of mouth in Minna, and that was it.

## Three questions

Parents ask the same three things before they enrol: what does this place actually stand for, how do the students do, and how do I start. So the site answers those in that order and then gets out of the way.

## Built for a bad connection

It also has to load on a bad connection, on a phone, on the first try. That shaped more decisions than anything aesthetic did.
