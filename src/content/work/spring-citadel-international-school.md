## Overview

Spring Citadel International School has been running in Minna, Niger State, since 2008, from nursery through to WAEC. It has more than 2,400 enrolled students.

I designed and built its website, so parents can finally judge the school online before they ever walk through the gate.

<a class="visit-btn" href="https://www.springcitadelschool.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Visit site &#8599;</a>

## Challenge

The school had nothing online a parent could judge it by. A Facebook page and word of mouth in Minna, and that was it.

Parents ask the same three things before they enrol: what does this place actually stand for, how do the students do, and how do I start. The site had to answer those in that order, and then get out of the way.

It also had to load on a bad connection, on a phone, on the first try. That shaped more decisions than anything aesthetic did.

## Outcome

The site opens with one clear line, "Where learning meets character, from nursery to WAEC", and two buttons: apply for admission, or explore the academics.

Below that it follows the three questions. The mission explains what the school stands for. Figures from school records, the WAEC pass rate, enrolment and years of excellence, show how students do. Enroll buttons sit throughout the page so starting is never more than a tap away. Parent testimonials, a photo gallery, a school portal link and a WhatsApp chat button round it out.

The result is a school that is easy to check out and easy to contact, even on a slow phone connection.
