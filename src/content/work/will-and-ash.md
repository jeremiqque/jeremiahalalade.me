## Overview

Will & Ash is a commercial law firm with offices in Lagos and Abuja. It advises governments, financial institutions, companies, entrepreneurs and private clients across Africa and beyond.

I designed and built its website: a calm, clear front door for a firm that wants to look established and still feel easy to approach.

<a class="visit-btn" href="https://www.will-ash.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Visit site &#8599;</a>

## Challenge

Law firm websites usually fall into one of two traps. They feel cold and stiff, or they try so hard to be friendly that they lose the weight a legal brand needs.

Will & Ash has to speak to very different people: a government agency looking at a public-private partnership, a bank checking regulation, and a private client making a first, nervous call. It also covers seven practice areas, from corporate law to the creative economy, so nobody should have to hunt for the one that fits them.

## Outcome

The site opens on a plain promise, "Built to protect what matters most", over a statue of Lady Justice, with a note that the first consultation is free and confidential.

From there the page walks a visitor through who the firm is, where its offices are and the key facts, then the seven practice areas in a numbered list that is easy to scan. A four-step section, from consultation to resolution, shows what working with the firm looks like. It ends with why clients choose the firm, answers to common questions and a clear way to book a consultation.

A refined serif headline set against clean, simple text carries the tone: established, but modern.
